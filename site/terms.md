These terms and community guidelines apply to DitMesh chat and groups on every target platform. The app asks you to accept them before chatting, and again after a material change.

## Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users

DitMesh has **no tolerance** for objectionable content or abusive behaviour.
You must not use DitMesh to send, share or encourage:

- harassment, bullying, threats or intimidation;
- hate speech or content that attacks people for who they are;
- sexual content involving minors, or any unsolicited sexual content;
- violent extremism, or content that promotes violence or self-harm;
- spam, scams, phishing or malware;
- anything that is illegal where you or the recipient live, or that infringes
  someone else's rights.

## Blocking

Only people you accept can message you; groups are joined by invitation or
group ID. **Block** anyone from a conversation, a group's member list, a
friend request or a group invite. Blocking removes them as a friend and hides
their messages, requests and invites on your device. Tox gives every group
member a separate key in each group, so blocking someone from a group's member
list hides them in that group. Manage blocked people under
**Me → Blocked people**.

DitMesh is peer to peer: there is no server, so nobody — including us — can
read conversations, remove messages from other people's devices or suspend
accounts. Blocking is the protection the app gives you; for illegal content,
contact the authorities where you live.

## Your responsibilities

- You are responsible for what you send and for keeping your identity, password
  and backups safe. We cannot recover a lost identity.
- You must be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age in your country) to use
  the chat features.
- Amateur-radio content (callsigns, Q-codes) is welcome; follow the etiquette
  of the band.
