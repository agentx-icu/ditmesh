### Capture sources

The 26 native-window captures for each of macOS, Linux and Windows come from the successful desktop jobs for DitMesh revision `c2cdf7d`. The captured Flutter content is 1280 × 645 on macOS and 1280 × 800 on Linux and Windows. [Desktop E2E run](https://github.com/agentx-icu/ditmesh/actions/runs/45850160991)

The same revision rendered [direct chat](compact/en/conversation.png) and [group chat](compact/en/group_conversation.png) from the real Flutter screens with demo conversations at a 430 × 932 phone viewport. These images show the app content area. The iPhone (1320 × 2868), iPad (2064 × 2752) and Android (823 × 1829) images in the table come from the previous device capture and may differ from this revision. [Phone layout rendering run](https://github.com/agentx-icu/ditmesh/actions/runs/45850160654)
